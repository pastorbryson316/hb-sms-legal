SMS Privacy Policy

Hartsfield Baptist text updates · Effective October 9, 2026

This policy explains how Jerry Bryson, operating as Hartsfield Baptist, handles personal information connected with the Hartsfield Baptist text-message program.

Information we collect

When you enroll in Hartsfield Baptist text updates, we collect the mobile phone number you use and your opt-in or opt-out status. We may also retain messages you send to the program, such as JOIN, YES, HELP, and STOP, along with records needed to deliver and manage the service.

How we use information

We use this information to:

  • manage your enrollment in the text-message program;
  • send event announcements, service reminders, and prayer requests;
  • respond to help requests; and
  • honor opt-out requests and maintain program records.

How information is shared

Information may be processed by communications service providers only as needed to operate and deliver the text-message program.

We do not sell or share your SMS opt-in data or personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

Your choices

You may opt out at any time by replying STOP. For help, reply HELP or call us at 229-474-8621.

Program details

Messages are sent approximately once a week, with additional prayer-request messages as needed. Message and data rates may apply. Mobile carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.
